Discover Lesotho

Welcome to Lesotho, the Mountain Kingdom. Explore spectacular highlands, experience Basotho culture, and discover the warm hospitality of its people.

Five Destinations to Explore

1. Maletsunyane Falls — Semonkong

Visit the impressive Maletsunyane Falls and enjoy the surrounding mountain scenery. Visitors can explore the area on foot, by bicycle, or on horseback.

2. Thaba Bosiu — Maseru District

Discover the historic mountain stronghold associated with King Moshoeshoe I and the founding of the Basotho nation.

3. Sehlabathebe National Park

Explore remote highland scenery, rock formations, wetlands, and wildlife in this protected area.

4. Katse Dam — Bokong

Take in the mountain views around Katse Dam and learn about the Lesotho Highlands Water Project.

5. Afriski Mountain Resort — Mahlasela

Enjoy a highland getaway with seasonal snow sports and year-round mountain activities.
